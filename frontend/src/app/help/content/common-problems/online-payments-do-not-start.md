# Online payments do not start

## Symptoms
"Pay now" fails, the guest cannot reach the payment page, or a message says the payout account is not configured.

## What to check
1. **Payout account.** Setup → Payments needs a verified bank account. An outlet may have its own (POS → Setup → Online payout account); otherwise the hotel's is used. See [Payment methods](help:getting-started/04-payments).
2. **Guest email.** The guest's profile needs an email address.
3. **Connection.** Payments cannot be taken while the app shows it is offline.
4. **Stuck payment.** A payment shown as pending can be checked again with **Verify** on the folio. A refund Paystack has not completed shows as waiting.

## Still stuck
Take the payment by cash or terminal for now and tell your administrator.
