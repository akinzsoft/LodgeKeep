# A tax is missing or wrong

## Symptoms
Bills carry no tax, or the wrong rate, or a mini-mart sale has the hotel tax.

## What to check
1. Setup → **Taxes**. A tax with no live version adds nothing; the screen shows a banner when the hotel tax is off.
2. **Dates.** A rate applies from its **effective from** date. A rate changed today does not alter older bills.
3. **Who can change it.** Only the super admin can add, switch off, reactivate or delete a tax.
4. **Applies to.** "All hotel charges" covers rooms and POS. "Supermarket sales only" covers only the supermarket till.

## Still stuck
A tax already used on bills cannot be deleted; switch it off from a date instead.
