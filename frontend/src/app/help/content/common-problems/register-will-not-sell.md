# The Register will not let me sell

## Symptoms
No outlet or terminal to choose, no menu items, items greyed out, or "not your tab".

## What to check
1. **Outlet.** It must be a selling outlet, not a store room, and you must be assigned to it (Staff → Outlets). Without any assignment you can use every outlet.
2. **Terminal.** The outlet needs one. Create it under POS → Setup.
3. **Menu items.** An item appears only if its category is carried by the outlet and it is marked available. Items that ran out of stock may be switched off.
4. **Someone else's tab.** Only the person who opened a tab can add to or settle it. Ask them to hand it over, or a manager can act with a reason.
5. **Card or transfer** needs the online payout account.

## Still stuck
Ask a manager to open POS → Setup and check the outlet's categories and items.
