# A refund or void asks for approval

## Symptoms
A dialog asks for a manager and a PIN, lists nobody to choose, or says the PIN is locked.

## What to check
1. **The approver sets a PIN.** Top-right menu → My Profile → Approval PIN. Managers without one are shown but cannot be chosen.
2. **The approver must be able to approve** at this property (managers, admins, super admins).
3. **Wrong guesses.** Five wrong PINs in 15 minutes lock that PIN for 15 minutes, and managers are alerted. The owner can reset it with their password.
4. **Time.** An approval works once and expires after 2 minutes. Ask again if you wait.
5. **Hotel folio refunds** use the cashier permission and do not need a PIN. Register card, supermarket and stock-override actions do.

## Still stuck
Ask your administrator which managers have a PIN set.
