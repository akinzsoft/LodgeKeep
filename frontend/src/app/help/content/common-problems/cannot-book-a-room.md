# I cannot book a room

## Symptoms
Availability shows zero, Book is refused, or no room can be chosen at check-in.

## What to check
1. **Setup is complete.** You need an active room type, rooms of that type, and a rate code. See [Room types, rooms and rates](help:getting-started/02-rooms-and-rates).
2. **Rooms are sellable.** Rooms that are archived, out of order, or flagged with an open housekeeping discrepancy do not count.
3. **Overbooking limit.** A night that is full is refused beyond the property's overbooking threshold.
4. **Check-in.** The room must be free and clean. A dirty room can be overridden only by someone who may do so.
5. **Checkout** is blocked while the folio still owes money.

## Still stuck
Ask your administrator to check Setup → Guided Setup for any step not ticked.
