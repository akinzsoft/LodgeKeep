# Staff, roles and approval PINs

## What it is for
Each person gets a role that decides what they can open. Refunds, voids and selling past stock need a **manager's approval PIN** typed at the till, so at least one manager must set a PIN before anyone needs it.

## Where to click
1. Staff (or Setup → **Users**) → **Invite**. The person accepts by email and sets a password.
2. Each approver: top-right menu → **My Profile** → **Approval PIN**.
3. Security options (the admin sign-in code): Setup → **Security**, super admin only.

## Fields that matter
1. **Email** and **role**. Roles are per property, so someone can be a manager at one and front desk at another.
2. The approval PIN is 6 digits, separate from the password, and only for people who can approve (managers and above).
3. Admins and super admins get an emailed sign-in code. Make sure the email settings (Setup → **Email**) deliver.

## You are done when
At least one person besides you is listed under Staff, and at least one manager shows a PIN set when a refund asks who approves.

Trouble? See [A refund or void asks for approval](help:common-problems/approval-pin-and-refunds).
