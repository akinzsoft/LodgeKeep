# A first test booking, check-in, charge, payment and check-out

## What it is for
Prove the whole loop works with one guest before real guests arrive. Use a made-up guest, and cancel or void afterwards.

## Where to click
1. Booking → **Availability**: search, pick a guest (add one if needed), **Book**. Payment can be taken at booking or later.
2. Booking → **Front Desk** → Arrivals → **Check in**, choosing a clean room.
3. **Cashiering**: open the guest's folio, **Post a charge**, then **Capture a payment** (cash is quickest).
4. Front Desk → In-House or Departures → **Check out**.

## Fields that matter
1. The room must be clean and free to check in. Housekeeping marks it clean.
2. Checkout is blocked while the folio has a balance owing, so pay it first.
3. Charges post to the current business date, with tax added automatically.

## You are done when
The guest is checked out with a zero balance and the room shows as dirty for housekeeping. The folio shows the room charges, the tax lines and your payment.

Trouble? See [I cannot book a room](help:common-problems/cannot-book-a-room).
