# Payment methods and the online payout account

## What it is for
Cash and card-terminal payments are recorded by staff and need no setup. **Online** payments (a guest paying on their phone, the Register's Paystack card or transfer, the supermarket's online payment) are paid out to a bank account you register here.

## Where to click
1. Setup → **Payments** for the hotel's payout account and the hotel's own card-terminal accounts.
2. POS → **Setup** → pick an outlet → **Online payout account** if an outlet banks to its own account. Without one it uses the hotel's account.

## Fields that matter
1. **Bank** and **account number**. Use the resolve button: the account name Paystack returns should be your business.
2. Terminal accounts are for records only: they let you match a terminal's settlement report. They move no money.
3. Guests need an email address on their profile to pay online.

## You are done when
Setup → Payments shows your account name and the last four digits, and a small online test payment goes through. If you only take cash and terminal payments, you can tick this step as not needed.

Trouble? See [Online payments do not start](help:common-problems/online-payments-do-not-start).
