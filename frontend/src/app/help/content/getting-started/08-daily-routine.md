# The daily routine

## What it is for
A short routine each day keeps cash, stock and the books correct. Night Audit is manual: nothing moves the business date for you.

## Where to click
1. Start of shift: POS → **Shifts** → open a shift on your terminal, entering the opening float.
2. During the day: take payments in the Register and Cashiering.
3. End of shift: POS → **Shifts** → close it by counting the cash. Expected cash is shown only after you submit the count.
4. End of day: Front desk checks out departures first, then a manager opens **Night Audit** and runs it.

## Fields that matter
1. Count cash honestly; the variance is recorded against the shift.
2. Night Audit closes one business day per run, posts the night's room charges, and moves the date forward. It will not run until that day has really ended in the property's timezone.
3. Open housekeeping discrepancies must be resolved before the audit can run.

## You are done when
The shift is closed, Night Audit shows a completed run for today's business date, and the top bar shows the next date.

Trouble? See [Night Audit will not run](help:common-problems/night-audit-will-not-run).
