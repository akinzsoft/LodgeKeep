# Who to contact

## What it is for
Knowing who to ask saves time when something blocks a guest or a sale.

## Where to click
Start with the guides in Help → **Common problems**. They cover the questions that come up most.

## Who to ask
1. **Your own administrator** (the person who signed up your hotel) for roles, PINs, outlets, prices and anything about your own settings.
2. **A manager on duty** for refunds, voids and overrides.
3. **Planmsys**, who runs LodgeKeep, for sign-in problems you cannot clear, billing, or something that looks like a fault. See the link in the footer of every page.

## Before you ask
Note the screen, what you pressed, the exact message, and the time. If a message shows a request id, include it.

## You are done when
You know who your administrator is and have their contact details written where the team can find them.
