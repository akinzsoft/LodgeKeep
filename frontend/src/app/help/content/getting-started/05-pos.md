# POS outlets, menu, terminals and QR codes

## What it is for
An **outlet** is a place that sells (a bar, a restaurant, a mini-mart). The **Register** needs an outlet, a **terminal** and **menu items** to sell. **QR codes** let guests order from their phones.

## Where to click
POS → **Setup**. Work down the outlet's cards: outlet, categories and menu items, terminals. Then POS → **QR codes** if you want guest ordering.

## Fields that matter
1. Outlet: **name**, **type**. A store room is for stock only and cannot sell.
2. Menu categories and items: **name**, **price**, **category**. An item shows at an outlet only when the outlet carries its category. Link stock items to an item so sales reduce stock.
3. Terminal: a **device name** for the till. A shift is opened on a terminal.
4. QR codes: one per table or room. Guests pay by card; staff accept or reject the order.
5. Staff who should only work at some outlets: Staff → **Outlets**.

## You are done when
POS → **Register** shows your outlet, a terminal, and the items you added, and you can open a tab.

Trouble? See [The Register will not let me sell](help:common-problems/register-will-not-sell).
